Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Worth It app for iPhone (“the app”), formerly called ReviewYou.

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers.

Your places

Your places, ratings, visits, notes and photos are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also saved in your own private iCloud storage using Apple's CloudKit, so they sync and are backed up. The developer cannot see them. Apple handles iCloud under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing with your partner

If you invite someone, Apple's iCloud sharing gives that person access to your shared places. Only people you invite can see them, and you can stop sharing at any time from the Us tab.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to center the map and show your nearest places. Your location stays on your device. The widget uses your last known location, also stored on your device.

Maps and search

Place search, addresses and directions use Apple Maps. Those requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

You can add a photo of a place with your camera or from your library. Only the photo you choose is used, and it's stored with your places.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
