Worth It

Save the places you go and answer one question together: was it worth it? Worth It was called ReviewYou before version 2.0.

Get help or report a problem

Open a ticket on the support page on GitHub. Tell us your device, iOS version and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Where did ReviewYou go? It's the same app with a new name. When you update, your saved places, ratings and photos come over automatically the first time you open it.

How does the Worth It verdict work? After a visit, each of you picks Worth It, Maybe or Skip It. If you agree, the place gets that stamp. If you don't, it's marked as a split decision. Your latest visit decides where a place stands.

What's a Quick Round? A stack of places still waiting on your verdict. Swipe right for worth it, left to skip it, or tap Not sure yet.

How do we share places between our phones? Both of you need Worth It installed and to be signed in to iCloud. On one phone, go to the Us tab and tap Invite, then send the invitation. When your partner opens it, you'll both see the same places, photos and verdicts.

How do I add a place? Tap + on the Map tab and search, or touch and hold anywhere on the map to drop a pin there.

Can I change the rating categories? Yes. In the Us tab, rename, reorder, remove or add categories.

What can I ask Siri? “Ask Worth It about Blue Agave”, “Worth it places near me”, “Show my Worth It map” and “Open my Want to Try list”. They're also in the Shortcuts app.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add Worth It. It shows your nearest Worth It places.
